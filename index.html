About Us

Founded in Beirut, Lebanon, Oplinx Consultancy and Smart Solutions delivers expert consulting and digital transformation services to businesses across various sectors. With a practical, agile, and deeply personalized approach, we transform complex challenges into actionable strategies and scalable results — guiding organizations through smart growth, digital integration, and operational excellence.
